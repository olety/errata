Errata turns your Claude Code and Codex session logs into a card run. Every monster is a pattern from your own history: the command you retried four times, the moment you stopped the agent and said "no, only this file". Every card is a rule. Your deck IS your global CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md, with a token budget, duplicates to fuse at the campfire, and a boss that replays the cases you held back. Apply writes the files with byte-exact backups and Undo. Nothing leaves the tab.
